# Asset provenance / 资源来源

The Sponza model, materials and textures were copied from the existing HybridURPDemo for this local example. Their original license and source attribution were not present in that copy. Confirm the original distribution and include its license/credits before redistributing these assets publicly; the rendering packages' licenses do not grant rights to the model or textures.

本地示例的 Sponza 模型、材质和贴图来自现有 HybridURPDemo。该副本未附带原始资源的授权与署名记录，公开分发这些资源前需确认原始来源，并补齐对应许可证及署名；渲染包的授权不涵盖模型和贴图。

Probe data, scene settings and demonstration scripts are maintained with this example. The four rendering packages are downloaded from [FraxinusGraphics](https://github.com/bluewhitebow/FraxinusGraphics) at the commit pinned in `Packages/manifest.json` and retain their own licenses and third-party notices.

探针数据、场景配置与演示脚本由此示例维护。四个渲染包按 `Packages/manifest.json` 固定的提交号从 [FraxinusGraphics](https://github.com/bluewhitebow/FraxinusGraphics) 下载，保留各自的许可证和第三方声明。
